Mindfulness for Chronic Pain

A seven-week journey toward healing

This seven week group is for anyone who suffers from pain and is interested in learning new ways to cope. Whether you have never meditated before, or you want to expand your practice, join us and:

  • Learn practical, mindfulness based skills for managing pain
  • Develop your own mindfulness practice for stress management
  • Explore the relationship between the mind and body
  • Begin developing a more peaceful relationship with your body
  • Connect with others who experience chronic pain
